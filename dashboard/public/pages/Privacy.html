Privacy Policy

Your financial data is sensitive. We treat it with the respect it deserves.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

To provide our expense tracking service, we collect the following information:

  • Telegram User Data: Your Telegram User ID and Username to associate your expenses with your account.
  • Chat Messages: The messages you send to the bot to log your expenses. These are temporarily processed by our LLM provider to extract the financial data.
  • Financial Data: Structured data extracted from your messages, including amounts, categories, dates, and people involved.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for providing you with the Money Mate service:

  • To maintain your personal ledger and track your spending.
  • To generate reports and analytics for you.
  • To improve our natural language understanding models (anonymized data only).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial data with third-party advertisers. Your messages are sent to an LLM provider (such as Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) via secure API exclusively for intent parsing. These providers do not use API data to train their public models.

4. Security

We implement standard security measures to protect your data. All communication between Telegram and our servers, and between our servers and our database, is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

5. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of all your data. You can clear your entire history by sending the /delete_account command to the bot. This action is irreversible.
