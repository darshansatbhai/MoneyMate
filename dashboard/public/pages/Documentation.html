Documentation

Everything you need to know about using Money Mate to track your expenses effortlessly.

Getting Started

Money Mate is an AI-powered expense tracking bot on Telegram. There is no app to install and no complicated forms to fill out. You simply chat with the bot just like you would with a friend.

  1. Open Telegram and search for @HeyMoneyMate_bot (or tap here).
  2. Send /start to initialize your account.
  3. Start sending your expenses in natural language.

Logging Expenses

You can use English, Hinglish, or Hindi to log your expenses. The AI automatically understands the amount, category, and context. You can also send a voice note or a photo of a bill.

Direct Spending

"Spent 250 on coffee and snacks"

"Uber ke liye 500 diye"

"Chai 20 aur samosa 30"

Income

"Salary aayi 45k"

"Got a refund of 1,200 from Amazon"

Lending & Borrowing

Money Mate also acts as a ledger to remember who owes you money — and whom you owe.

"Rahul ko 1000 udhaar diye"

"Gave 50 for lunch to Aman"

"Priya se 300 liye"

Settling Up

When someone pays you back, just tell the bot.

"Rahul paid back 500"

"Aman ne 200 wapas kiye"

Commands

  • /start Initialize or restart the bot.
  • /report Get a summary of your spending for the week or month.
  • /ledger View pending balances (who owes you, and who you owe).
  • /export Download your data as a CSV file.
  • /help Show this documentation in chat.
  • /delete_account Permanently delete all your data. This cannot be undone.

Correcting Mistakes

If the AI misunderstands an entry or you made a typo, you can simply tap the inline "Undo" button attached to the bot's reply, or just type Undo last entry.
