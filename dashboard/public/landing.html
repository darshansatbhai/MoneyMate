How it works

Up and running in 10 seconds.

No forms, no spreadsheets, no new app. If you can send a message, you can track money.

STEP 01

Open the bot

Search @HeyMoneyMate_bot in Telegram, or tap any “Open Telegram” button on this page.

STEP 02

Say hello

Send /start. Your private ledger is created instantly — no email or password.

/start
STEP 03

Just chat

Type, speak or snap a bill. Money Mate figures out the amount, category and people.
